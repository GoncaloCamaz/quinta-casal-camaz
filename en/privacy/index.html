Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit this website or contact us, what it is used for and what rights you have. We have tried to keep it simple.

Who is responsible

Quinta Casal Camaz, Rua de Aveleda 602, 4485-017 Aveleda, Vila do Conde, Portugal.
Email: geral@quintacasalcamaz.pt

What data we process

When you contact us. If you message us on WhatsApp, email us or call us, we receive the data you send: usually your name, your contact details and information about the event, such as the intended date, the type of celebration and the number of guests.

When you visit the website. This website has no forms, sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising tools. To serve the website and protect it from abuse, the services that host and deliver it (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) receive technical data about your connection, such as your IP address and browser type. These services may use cookies that are strictly necessary for security.

Map

The map in the "Getting here" section is an image hosted on this website (data © OpenStreetMap) and sends no data to third parties. If you use the "Open in Google Maps" or "View on Google Maps" links, you are taken to Google's website, which processes your data under Google's privacy policy.

What we use the data for

  • Answering your enquiries, booking visits and preparing proposals, at your request.
  • Organising the event, if you go on to hire our services.
  • Meeting legal obligations, such as invoicing.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with the providers we need to operate, such as our website hosting and email services, and only as far as necessary. When you contact us on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook, those platforms also process the conversation data under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep data for as long as needed to answer your request and follow up on the contact. If there is a contract, we keep what the legal retention periods require, namely for tax purposes. After that, the data is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its processing, for data portability, or object to its processing. Just write to geral@quintacasalcamaz.pt.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD) at www.cnpd.pt.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here, with its update date.
