Since 1995

A story of three generations

Quinta Casal Camaz began as a farmhouse, long before it opened its doors to weddings and celebrations.

It was in 1995 that everything began to change. For a family wedding, some of the farmhouse's spaces were prepared to host the celebration. That transformation gave rise to the venue's first hall and, with it, a new way of bringing the house to life.

Over the following decades, the venue grew alongside the family. The first hall took on a new purpose, the current main hall was built, and the different spaces were gradually adapted into what Quinta Casal Camaz is today.

More than 30 years later, now in its third generation, the venue keeps evolving without leaving behind the legacy that brought it here.

Spaces that complement each other.

The main hall is the heart of every celebration. Next to it, the venue's original hall serves as a complementary space, allowing different moments to be created throughout the event.

Getting here

Close to the city, surrounded by tranquility.

Quinta Casal Camaz is located in Aveleda, Vila do Conde, just minutes from Porto Airport, with quick access via the A28.

By car Recommended access via the A28, exit 12 (Lavra / Aveleda).
By metro Vilar do Pinheiro station just minutes away.
From the airport About 2.5 km from Porto Airport.
Parking Private and free on-site parking, with reserved spaces for guests with reduced mobility.
