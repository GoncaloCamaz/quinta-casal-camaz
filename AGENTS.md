# AGENTS.md — Quinta Casal Camaz

This file provides persistent context to AI assistants and development agents working in this repository. The goal is to avoid generic answers, preserve the real identity of Quinta Casal Camaz, and prevent business data from being invented.

## 1. Project overview

Quinta Casal Camaz is a family-owned farm/estate for weddings and other celebrations, located in Aveleda, in the municipality of Vila do Conde, Portugal.

Events-related activity started in 1995. The venue has been changed, maintained, and modernized over time, while keeping rustic and natural elements.

This digital project has two main goals:

1. Create a modern institutional website to promote the venue, the halls, the gardens, the location, and the contacts.
2. Support the broader physical and commercial modernization process of the venue, making it more comfortable, elegant, contemporary, and visually appealing.

The website should help turn visitors into leads and, ideally, into in-person visits to the venue.

### Project phases

This project is being delivered in two phases:

1. **Phase 1 — Coming-soon site (intermediate step).** A single, elegant page published before the final site is ready. Its main purpose is to let visitors reach the venue right away through direct links to social media, email, and phone/WhatsApp contact, while the full institutional site is being built. See section 10.
2. **Phase 2 — Final institutional site.** The full multi-section site described throughout this document (sections 1–13), replacing the coming-soon page once ready.

Agents must always check which phase is currently active before making changes, and must not merge phase 1 and phase 2 content together.

## 4. Brand positioning

The brand should convey:

- Nature.
- Elegance.
- Discreet luxury.
- Family tradition.
- Modernization.
- Closeness.
- Tranquility.
- Authenticity.
- Meaningful celebrations.

Avoid an overly classical, baroque, ostentatious, or corporate-hotel-like aesthetic. The intended luxury is warm, natural, and contemporary.

The core idea is a farm/estate with rural soul that is evolving into a more modern and premium experience without losing its identity.

## 5. Visual direction

### Recommended palette

Prioritize:

- Deep forest green.
- Olive green and sage green.
- Warm beige.
- Cream and warm white.
- Natural wood.
- Gold/champagne used sparingly.
- Matte black in small details.

Avoid:

- Overly saturated greens.
- Excessive bright gold.
- Clinical white.
- Dominant cold grays.
- Neon colors.

### Typography

Prefer the combination of:

- An elegant serif for headings.
- A modern, legible sans-serif for text, navigation, and forms.

Suitable examples: Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, or similar for headings; Manrope, DM Sans, Inter, or similar for interface and body text.

### Photography

Photographs are the main commercial asset of the website.

Priorities:

- Halls set up for events.
- Gardens and green paths.
- Patios and outdoor areas at dusk.
- Rustic architecture, wood, stone, and warm lighting.
- Wide shots that convey space and atmosphere.
- Real photographs of the venue whenever possible.

Do not use AI-generated images as if they were real photographs of the venue. Renders or concepts must be clearly identified as proposals, visualizations, or future projects.

## 6. Tone of voice

Write in **European Portuguese (pt-PT)**.

The tone should be:

- Elegant, but approachable.
- Emotional, but not melodramatic.
- Confident, but not overstated.
- Warm and welcoming.
- Clear and commercially useful.

Prefer phrases like:

- "Celebrações rodeadas de natureza." ("Celebrations surrounded by nature.")
- "Um espaço com história, pensado para o presente." ("A space with history, designed for the present.")
- "Marque uma visita à quinta." ("Book a visit to the venue.")
- "Onde a natureza recebe os seus melhores momentos." ("Where nature welcomes your best moments.")
- "Tradição rural, conforto contemporâneo." ("Rural tradition, contemporary comfort.")

Avoid empty clichés such as:

- "O casamento dos seus sonhos" ("The wedding of your dreams") repeated in every section.
- "Experiência única e inesquecível" ("A unique and unforgettable experience") without concrete information.
- "Luxo incomparável" ("Unmatched luxury").
- Absolute claims about being the best venue in the region.

## 8. Conversion goal

The main goal of the website is not just digital visits. It is to encourage potential clients to:

1. Book a visit.
2. Ask about availability.
3. Request information/a quote.
4. Get in touch via WhatsApp, phone, email, or the form.

The recommended contact hierarchy is:

1. Book a visit / WhatsApp.
2. Call.
3. Email.
4. Form.
5. How to get there.

The form should be short and ask only for useful information:

- Name.
- Email.
- Phone, optional or recommended.
- Event type.
- Expected date.
- Approximate number of guests.
- Message.

## 9. Rules for forms and security

Frontend validation is only for user experience. Any endpoint must validate again on the server or an external service.

Recommendations:

- Validation with native HTML and JavaScript.
- Zod or a similar library when the project has a JavaScript/TypeScript build.
- Anti-spam honeypot.
- Cloudflare Turnstile when a dedicated endpoint exists.
- Rate limiting on the endpoint.
- Field size limits.
- Do not expose SMTP keys, API keys, or secrets in the browser.
- Do not use `mailto:` as the final form solution.
- Clear success and error messages.
- Privacy consent when personal data is collected.

## 12. Performance

- Convert photographs to WebP or AVIF when appropriate.
- Keep originals out of the public bundle when not needed.

- Lazy-load below the fold.
- Prioritize only the main hero image.
- Avoid heavy libraries for simple animations.
- Ensure good performance on mobile networks.

## 13. Physical state and modernization vision

The venue's project includes ideas to modernize the hall and outdoor areas.

Intended direction for the hall:

- Preserve the existing ceiling and wood elements.
- Integrate a new area with a white plasterboard ceiling.
- Create an elegant transition between rustic and modern.
- Use indirect lighting and aligned spotlights.
- Prefer warm lighting, approximately 2700K–3000K.
- Walls in warm beige or taupe.
- Light-colored wood-effect flooring.
- Matte black details used sparingly.
- Keep real pillars and structural constraints.

Intended direction for the outdoor areas:

- Expand and improve the gardens.
- Create more lawn and photogenic areas.
- Improve nighttime lighting.
- There is a conceptual proposal for a small pool integrated into the new garden; do not treat it as existing without confirmation.
